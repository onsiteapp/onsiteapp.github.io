Onsite — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

Onsite ("the app") is a scheduling and client-management app for mobile service professionals (hairstylists, massage therapists, groomers, trainers, and similar businesses). This policy explains what data the app touches, why, and where it goes.

The short version: Onsite is local-only. There is no Onsite server, no operator account, and no analytics or advertising SDK. Your business data lives on your own device.

Who this covers

This policy is written for the operator — the person who installs Onsite to run their business. Onsite has no separate app or account for the operator's clients; clients are contacts the operator manages inside their own copy of the app.

Data Onsite accesses, and why

DataWhy Onsite asks for itWhere it goes
Contacts (read-only) One-time import of your phone contacts into your Onsite client list, only when you tap "Import from Contacts." Copied into the app's local database on your device. Never uploaded, never read again unless you re-import.
Calendar (read-only) Import personal events from your device calendar so Onsite can show them alongside your bookings and avoid scheduling over them. Only requested when you tap "Import." Copied into the app's local database. Onsite never writes to or modifies your calendar.
Location (foreground only) Automatically marking an appointment complete when you leave a client's location, and an optional "On My Way" live ETA you start manually for a specific trip. Used on-device. Onsite never collects location in the background. Coordinates for an active ETA trip are sent to a routing service — see Third-Party Services below.
Notifications Appointment reminders. Generated and shown entirely on your device (local notifications).
Photos/media (optional) Branding — your logo appears in the app's header, if you choose to upload one. Chosen by you from your device's photo picker; copied into the app's private storage. Never uploaded anywhere.

Onsite does not request background location, does not read your SMS messages, and does not read your contacts or calendar except during an import you explicitly trigger.

Data Onsite does NOT collect

  • No user accounts, no login, no password.
  • No analytics SDK, no crash-reporting SDK, no advertising SDK.
  • No data is sold, rented, or used for advertising — Onsite shows no ads.
  • No client data is ever transmitted to a server operated by Onsite, because no such server exists. All appointment, client, and revenue records stay in a local database on your device.

Third-party services

Because Onsite has no server of its own, a few features call free, no-account, third-party services directly from your device:

  • OpenStreetMap (Nominatim / Photon) — turns an address you type into map coordinates, so the app can calculate drive times and show a pin. Only the address text is sent; no client name or other details.
  • OSRM (router.project-osrm.org) — calculates drive time/ETA between two points for the optional "On My Way" feature. Only two coordinate pairs are sent.
  • Google Maps — tapping "Get Directions" opens the Google Maps app on your phone with an address you chose. This hands off to another app on your phone; Onsite has no Google Maps API key and sends Google no analytics.
  • Your phone's own Messaging app — tapping a message action opens your phone's default SMS app with a prefilled message for you to review and send. Onsite never sends the message itself.

None of these services receive your name, your business identity, or your client's identity — only the specific coordinates or address text needed to answer that one request.

(Planned, not yet active: an optional Twilio SMS integration where the operator connects their own Twilio account. This page will be updated before that feature ships.)

Where your data lives, and backup

All business data is stored in a local database on your device. Two things can copy it off the device, both under your control: your phone's own OS backup (Google Account backup / iCloud), if enabled, and a manual export you trigger yourself to a location you choose. Onsite has no way to access, read, or restore your data remotely.

Data deletion

Uninstalling Onsite deletes all locally stored data on that device. Because Onsite keeps no server-side copy, removing the app is the deletion.

Children's privacy

Onsite is a business tool for adult service professionals and is not directed at, or knowingly used to collect data from, children.

Security

Onsite relies on your device's own operating-system security. Because there is no Onsite account or password, an optional App Lock feature (off by default) can require your phone's biometric or a PIN before the app's contents are shown.

Changes to this policy

If what Onsite collects or how changes, this page will be updated and the "Last updated" date above will change accordingly.
