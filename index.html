Scheduling built for people who work at their clients' locations.

Onsite is a scheduling app for hairstylists, massage therapists, mobile groomers, personal trainers, and anyone who travels to appointments instead of running a storefront. No cloud account. No ads. Your data stays on your phone.

Google Play — coming soon

What Onsite does

📅 Smart scheduling

Day, week, and month views. Recurring visits on a cadence you define, and Smart Picks suggests open slots based on drive-time efficiency and when each client is due.

👥 Client management

Import contacts from your phone, a vCard, or a CSV. Track service history, notes, and how often each client needs you back.

🗺️ Drive-time aware

See distance to your next stop and get directions in one tap. Optional live "On My Way" ETA you start per trip — never tracked in the background.

💰 Revenue tracking

Totals by day, week, month, quarter, or year, broken down by service. Mileage estimates for tax season.

🎨 Your brand

Upload your logo and set your own colors throughout the app.

🔒 Optional App Lock

Add a PIN or biometric lock so a client browsing your phone can't see your other clients.
