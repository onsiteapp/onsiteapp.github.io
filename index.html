What Onsite does

🎯 Smart Picks: less driving, more clients

Onsite suggests the days and times that fit the route you are already driving, so you spend less on gas and time between clients. You set how far you will drive between stops, and Smart Picks fills the gaps inside that zone while keeping each client's due date in view.

  • See the real cost of every day. A week-at-a-glance strip shows how many minutes a visit would add to your drive, with the best day starred. A day on the far side of town is labelled as such, so an empty day never looks better than it is.
  • Two lists, so you choose the trade. "Best for your route" keeps your driving tight. A second list favours the client, whether they are overdue or not yet due, and tells you plainly what it costs: "3 days sooner, adds about 12 min of driving."
  • Rescheduling that makes sense. Moving a visit suggests the days closest to the one you had, not just the next open slot.

📅 Smart scheduling

Day, week, and month views. Recurring visits on a cadence you define, so you always know who is due back.

👥 Client management

Import contacts from your phone, a vCard, or a CSV. Track service history, notes, and how often each client needs you back.

🗺️ Drive-time aware

See distance to your next stop and get directions in one tap, in Google Maps, Waze, or your phone's own maps app. Optional live "On My Way" ETA you start per trip — never tracked in the background.

🧭 Route planning

Pick the clients you want to see and Onsite puts them in the shortest order, around the visits you've already booked. Save the day to your calendar, then open it in Maps a few stops at a time.

💰 Revenue tracking

Totals by day, week, month, quarter, or year, broken down by service. Mileage estimates for tax season.

🔒 Optional App Lock

Add a PIN or biometric lock so a client browsing your phone can't see your other clients.
