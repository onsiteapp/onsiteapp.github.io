Want your clients to book themselves?

That is Book It, the booking app Onsite grew out of: the scheduling you see in Onsite (calendar, clients, drive-time-aware booking, revenue), plus a web version your clients use too, set up for your business. It adds:

  • Client self-booking. Each client gets a private link, sees your open times and books, reschedules or cancels within your rules.
  • Day-before text reminders. A text to everyone coming tomorrow, sent through Twilio. Clients reply YES, CANCEL or RESCHEDULE and your schedule updates itself.
  • Running-late texts to the clients you choose, in one tap.
  • Voice commands. Built so you can keep booking by speaking, the way you would with Siri: book, cancel, mark paid and add notes.
  • Works offline. Changes sync across your phone and tablet when you are back online, deletes can be undone, and backups run automatically every day.

It is a separate service that we set up for you and tailor to how you work. Before anything goes live we agree in writing who owns your client data and who can see it.

It is new for client booking: the day-before texts have run with real clients, but client self-booking has been built and thoroughly tested without yet taking bookings from real clients, so we are looking for a first business to set it up with. Interested? Email onsitegeoschedule@gmail.com.
