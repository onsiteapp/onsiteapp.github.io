Where Onsite comes from

Onsite grew out of a booking app we built alongside a working mobile hairstylist, using her real client list and her real week. We ran it next to her day and found the problems that only show up in real use. She books mostly by voice with Siri, so we tried to let her keep doing that and have everything stay in sync with the app. Two ways to book meant two versions of the truth, and that was the hard part. It cost us her trust early on, and we fixed it release by release until she could rely on it.

Onsite is the scheduling core of that work, rebuilt as a private, phone-only app for any mobile service professional.

What came across

That app went through more than 230 releases, and its QA log records over 50 bugs found and fixed. Onsite starts from what those taught us instead of rediscovering them.
